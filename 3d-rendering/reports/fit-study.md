## Limitations

- Beauty geometry is authored fitted visualization, not calibrated simulation output.
- Native cloth has large local strains; material settings are uncalibrated isotropic approximations.
- Postures use geometric bends, not a musculoskeletal rig.
- Pouch collision uses a rigid ellipsoid without flange, outlet or fluid.
- Slip-on extension, comfort, retention, flange/outlet clearance, recovery and laundering require physical samples.
