## Film and larger scenes

The [versioned release](https://github.com/conley21p/ElevenForwardBandDesign/releases/tag/r3-patterns-and-3d) provides:

- [1080p review film](https://github.com/conley21p/ElevenForwardBandDesign/releases/download/r3-patterns-and-3d/assembly-1080p.mp4)
- [Native 4K final film](https://github.com/conley21p/ElevenForwardBandDesign/releases/download/r3-patterns-and-3d/assembly-4k.mp4)
- [Optional synchronized captions](video/assembly-captions.srt)
- Four native cloth scenes: 0%, 5% and 10% exploratory ease at 5 mm spacing, plus the 5% candidate at 2.5 mm.

Download checksums and exact release URLs are recorded in [release-assets.json](release-assets.json). The film is 40 seconds at 24 fps, without audio. The invisible-wearer view preserves the same fitted product geometry as the visible torso view.

**Physical fit and material calibration remain unverified.** The beauty scene is authored fitted visualization; separate native cloth studies have large local strains and do not qualify the pattern for production. Actual wearer measurements, seam/fabric samples, retention, access, slip-on extension, depth and laundering checks are required before physical approval.
