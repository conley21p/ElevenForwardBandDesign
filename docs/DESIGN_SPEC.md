## Geometry

- #1 has a centered 12-inch pouch zone. Its upper and lower edges each add 0.5 inch relative to the 7-inch base, for an 8-inch maximum. Expansion is integral; there is no separate outer front panel or gusset.
- #2a has a flange clearance cutout on its upper left and a straight upper-right edge. #2b is reflected vertically, with its cutout on the lower left. Both are required pieces, not alternative orientations. Left means left in the diagram; anatomical placement is not specified.
- The support cutouts are conceptual curves to be fitted to the selected bag/flange. Earlier discussion of generic cutout measurements is superseded; do not treat those as finalized specifications.
- #3 uses two identical rectangular panels, each 36 × 2 inches. Both material and cut/shape match. The drawing intentionally shows only one copy, marked Qty 2.
- #4 is oriented vertically at center back. Its 7-inch edges join the 7-inch ends of #1. 34 + 2 = 36 inches nominally before seam take-up and stretch adjustments.

## Assembly intent

1. Develop the interior pocket using #2a below and #2b above the horizontal reference bag. Align the left cutouts around the flange. Determine attachment seams and access using the actual bag.
2. Sew #1 ends to opposite vertical edges of #4 to form the body loop, placing #4 at center back and the pouch zone at front.
3. Attach #1's upper edge to one #3 and its lower edge to the second identical #3. Continue the edge treatment over the back insert as established in the final pattern.
4. Ease and shape the excess fabric around the pouch zone into the straight support bands to provide outward pouch volume. Flat geometry alone does not establish the resulting volume; sample construction is required.
5. Resolve #7 closure placement and the final closing method before producing a sewing pattern. The retained closure note is not a completed mechanical construction.

## Material and finish intent

Smooth matte black exterior, comfortable stretch recovery, and moisture management. #3 has plain faces, minimal visible stitching, and matching black thread, with no ribbing, gathering, exposed contrasting elastic, logo, or bulky seam ridge specified. A sports-bra underband reference informed this finish. Its exact composition and manufacturing construction were not established; polyester–spandex is the proposed material, not a verified claim about that reference.

The request originally referred to Dri-FIT-style moisture management. No branded fabric sourcing, licensing, certification, performance, stretch percentage, fabric weight, or fiber ratio is established. Select materials and test the sample against the intended use.
